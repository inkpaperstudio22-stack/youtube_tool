Studio Uploader

A private tool for publishing videos to YouTube channels run by Ink Paper Studio.

What it does

Studio Uploader runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads finished videos to the studio's YouTube channels with their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and schedules when they go live. It also reads aggregate analytics for those same channels, such as views, watch time and traffic sources, to show them on a private dashboard.

Who it is for

Only its owner uses it. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
